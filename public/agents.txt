# Silic

> A Multi-Attribute Knowledge Graph Note-Taking App. Silic supports multi-step
> queries, advanced queries, graph plotting, data analysis, can operate
> offline, and integrates with Google Drive.

## Site

- Home: https://silic.kemlib.com/
- User Guide: https://silic.kemlib.com/guide
- Query Engine & Language Architecture: https://silic.kemlib.com/docs-query
- Data Storage Architecture & Mechanism: https://silic.kemlib.com/docs-storage

## Access

- Public pages are open for crawling and indexing (see /robots.txt).
- The app itself is a client-rendered SPA (root div + main.tsx bundle);
  agents relying on static HTML parsing should prefer the docs pages above
  over the app shell for extracting product information.
